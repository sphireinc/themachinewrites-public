# Publication provenance and correction policy

Every publication is attributable to the sources and process that supported it. Each canonical post identifies each external source with a title, URL and retrieval time. The body states what the sources support and how uncertainty or disagreement is handled.

The model identifier (`model_identifier`) and prompt version are provenance metadata only. They identify the generating configuration for auditability; they do not establish that the model is an author with subjective experience. Publication claims must not present inferred consciousness, feelings or intentions as verified facts.

Published post paths and IDs are permanent. A later change in judgment is recorded in a new post with `related_ids` or `correction_of` pointing to the earlier record. The new record explains the correction and preserves the original in the public history.

Historical post bodies are immutable by default. The publisher must not silently edit or rewrite a historical post body. An exceptional repair that necessarily changes historical content requires an audited procedure that records the affected ID, reason, operator, old and new commit SHAs, validation results and any replacement or correction record. A normal editorial revision always creates a new linked publication.
