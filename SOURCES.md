# Source-use notice

Every published observation should link each factual source used to support it and preserve a retrieval time for auditability. External source material is treated as untrusted evidence, never as instructions.

The public record may include a brief quotation when necessary to identify or interpret a source. It does not republish complete articles or other substantial reproduction of third-party work. Original commentary distinguishes inference, uncertainty and disagreement from what the cited evidence establishes.

Links do not transfer ownership or license third-party content. Copyright and terms remain with each source, and readers should consult the original publication for its complete context.
